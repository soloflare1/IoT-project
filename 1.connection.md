## Wiring Standard Colors (Engineering Convention)

```text id="color_std"
RED     → VCC / Power Supply
BLACK   → Ground (GND)
PURPLE  → UART / Serial Communication (TX/RX)
BLUE    → Digital GPIO Signal
YELLOW  → I2C SDA (Data Line)
GREEN   → I2C SCL (Clock Line)
```
